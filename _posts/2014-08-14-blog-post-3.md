One of the central outputs of my PhD work is now out: <em>The Design and Implementation of a Graph-Based P2P Data Storage Service</em>, published in <em>Future Internet</em>.

The paper focuses on how peer-to-peer storage ideas can be combined with graph-based data management to support more decentralised information systems. This line of work sits at the core of my doctoral research and captures the question that keeps guiding it: what changes when we stop assuming that data integration has to be centrally controlled?

The publication is available via DOI: <https://doi.org/10.3390/fi18010009>.
