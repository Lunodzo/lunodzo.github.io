I started a new chapter at the Technical University of Denmark as a Research Assistant at DTU Wind and Energy Systems.

My current work spans two applied research tracks:

- `3PhaseInsight`, where I contribute to data applications for smart-meter data and platform orchestration.
- `AI-EFFECT`, where I support AI workflow implementation, data pipelines, and open-source platform deployment.

This move gives me a stronger applied setting for my ongoing work on data management in energy systems, while keeping a clear line to my doctoral research on decentralised approaches to data integration.
