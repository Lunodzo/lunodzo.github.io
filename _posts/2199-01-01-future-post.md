I presented our work on a decentralised relational data model for reliability studies of medium-voltage cables at IEEE PES ISGT Europe 2024.

The work connects modelling choices to real reliability-analysis needs in power systems, and it strengthened an ongoing line of research that later developed into related publications on CIM-based and decentralised data models.

Conference presentations remain one of the best ways to test ideas early, compare design assumptions, and refine how technical contributions are explained across disciplines.
