Our survey paper, <em>Big data energy systems: A survey of practices and associated challenges</em>, has been published in <em>Computer Science Review</em>.

The paper reviews how data is currently managed across energy-system settings and where major technical and organisational constraints still remain. It also reflects the broader motivation behind much of my research: better data engineering is necessary if digital energy systems are going to become interoperable, scalable, and useful in practice.

This publication draws together several strands of work that have shaped my recent research, especially around system design, integration, and the realities of modern data platforms.
