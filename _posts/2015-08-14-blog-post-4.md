My research stay with the InnoCyPES project in Italy has come to an end.

I joined the University of Salento in 2023 as an Early-Stage Researcher under the Marie Sklodowska-Curie-funded InnoCyPES network. During that period I worked on large-scale data management and integration for energy systems, collaborated across partner institutions, and deepened my work on peer-to-peer data management.

The experience shaped both my technical direction and my understanding of how collaborative doctoral research can connect infrastructure, power systems, and data engineering.
