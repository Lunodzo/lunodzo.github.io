Researcher · Data engineering · Database systems

Designing data systems for the energy transition, and asking “what if we decentralise?”

I am a researcher at DTU Wind and Energy Systems and a PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Instituto Superior Técnico, University of Lisbon, where I was a Marie Skłodowska-Curie fellow with InnoCyPES. I work on data modelling and database systems for modern data platforms, with a focus on compliance and future-proof design, and on decentralised data management for cyber-physical energy systems.

Research interests

Data modelling & database systems

Relational and graph data models, CIM-based models for grid reliability, and compliant, future-proof data platforms.

Decentralised data management

Peer-to-peer storage and integration services, and distributed systems for data shared across organisations.

Energy data engineering

Big data in energy systems, smart-meter data pipelines, and data platforms for distribution grids.

Cybersecurity

Detection of attacks on wireless networks and mobile devices, AI-driven security, and the psychology of security.
